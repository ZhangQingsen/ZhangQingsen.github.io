My research is on 3D face reconstruction from wearable cameras, and I previously worked as a machine vision engineer, building C++/OpenCV inspection systems for lithium-battery production lines. I care about computer vision that works in real industrial environments.

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in Prof. Yincheng Jin's lab at Binghamton, working on wearable and egocentric vision (expected graduation May 2028).
