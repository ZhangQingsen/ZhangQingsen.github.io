Qingsen Zhang

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Binghamton University

I work on 3D computer vision, generative modeling, and wearable sensing. My current research focuses on recovering structured 3D geometry from severely constrained egocentric observations.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I worked on machine-vision software and production-site deployment for industrial automation systems. I am particularly interested in connecting learning-based vision research with reliable systems that operate under real-world constraints.
